## Project Showcase

### Apple Stock Price Analysis (2018–2020)

**Research Question**: What are the price trends and volatility characteristics of Apple stock from July 2018 to June 2020?

**Visualization**:

![Apple Stock Price Trend](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/zloi287591443-hue/data-science-project/main/price_trend.png)

**Key Findings**: The stock yielded a total return of 100.22% over the period. However, it experienced high volatility during the COVID-19 shock in March 2020, with a max daily drop of -12.80% and a local price low of $223.76. The 50-day and 200-day moving averages show a clear trend reversal and recovery.
